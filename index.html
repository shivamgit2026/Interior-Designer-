Studio Philosophy

Tactile Minimalism & Uncompromising Precision

At Valenti & Co., we reject transient design trends in pursuit of timeless architectural permanence. Every environment is sculpted as a unified holistic sanctuary.

Founded across New York and Paris, our atelier blends French classical proportions with American architectural ambition. From single-slab Tuscan travertine islands to bespoke acoustic millwork crafted by third-generation cabinetmakers in Brianza, our material integrity is unyielding.

We operate exclusively on a limited commission model, accepting only ten bespoke residential projects each year to guarantee direct, uncompromising partner-level stewardship from initial sketch to turnkey white-glove handover.

The Discipline

Four-Phase Architectural Methodology

Our rigorous execution framework ensures unswerving aesthetic fidelity, transparent timelines, and seamless white-glove coordination.

01

Vision & Spatial Anatomy

We decipher your daily lifestyle rituals, analyze solar angles, conduct laser site scans, and develop initial architectural diagrams and space plans.

02

Photorealistic 3D & Materiality

We build photorealistic 3D virtual walkthroughs, present physical stone and fabric trays, and finalize complete construction drawing sets.

03

Artisan Fabrication

Direct commissioning of Italian stone quarries, bespoke European joinery, and custom lighting fixtures with rigorous shop drawing approvals.

04

Turnkey White-Glove Handover

Seamless installation, site management, fine art mounting, acoustic tuning, and styling for a fully realized move-in ready sanctuary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Key considerations regarding our commission model, timelines, geographic scope, and turnkey execution.

Due to the bespoke nature and intensive partner-level involvement in our commissions, we typically take on projects starting at 2,000 square feet with full turnkey budgets from $350,000 upwards. We evaluate smaller heritage spaces on a case-by-case architectural merit basis.

Yes. Approximately 40% of our commissions are located internationally, including London, Geneva, Saint-Barthélemy, Milan, and Miami. We maintain established logistics pipelines and vetted master general contractor partnerships across Europe and North America.

Discretion is paramount. We routinely operate under strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). All digital site documentation is encrypted, and published case studies are stripped of floorplans and identifying details unless explicitly authorized by the client.

A full interior architecture commission typically spans 6 to 12 months. Conceptual design and 3D approvals require 6 to 8 weeks, permit filings and custom millwork fabrication 10 to 14 weeks, followed by on-site construction and white-glove installation.
